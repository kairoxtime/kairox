# KAIROX — Mission Process  
Institutional Framework for Humanitarian Mission Execution, Verification, and Reporting

---

## Purpose of This Document
The Mission Process defines how humanitarian missions are proposed, approved, executed, verified, and audited within the KAIROX ecosystem.  
It ensures that all missions:

- Align with KAIROX’s humanitarian purpose  
- Follow transparent and accountable procedures  
- Use resources responsibly  
- Produce measurable and verifiable impact  
- Maintain security and operational integrity

## Mission Principles
All missions must adhere to the following principles:

1. **Humanitarian Impact** — Missions must directly improve human well‑being.  
2. **Transparency** — All mission stages must be publicly documented.  
3. **Verification** — Impact must be measurable and independently verifiable.  
4. **Security** — Missions must minimize operational, financial, and reputational risk.  
5. **Efficiency** — Resources must be used responsibly and effectively.

---

## Mission Lifecycle

### 1. Mission Proposal
A mission begins with a formal proposal submitted using the official template:  
→ [KAIROX_PROPOSAL_TEMPLATE.md](/KAIROX_PROPOSAL_TEMPLATE.md)

The proposal must include:
- Mission objective  
- Target population  
- Expected impact  
- Budget and resource requirements  
- Timeline  
- Risks and mitigations  
- Verification plan  

Naming convention:  
`proposal-short-title-YYYY-MM-DD.md`

---

### 2. Mission Review
Governance Maintainers evaluate the mission proposal for:

- Alignment with humanitarian purpose  
- Feasibility and operational clarity  
- Budget justification  
- Security considerations  
- Verification methodology  

Maintainers may request revisions before approval.

---

### 3. Mission Approval
Once approved through the governance process:

- The mission is assigned an official status: **Approved**  
- Treasury allocation is authorized (if applicable)  
- Execution planning begins  

Approval rules follow the Governance Framework:  
→ [GOVERNANCE_FRAMEWORK.md](/GOVERNANCE_FRAMEWORK.md)

---

### 4. Mission Execution
The mission team carries out the approved plan.  
Execution requirements:

- Follow the approved budget  
- Maintain communication with Governance Maintainers  
- Document progress with photos, receipts, logs, or field reports  
- Report any deviations or risks immediately  

Treasury disbursements may occur in phases based on milestones.

---

### 5. Field Verification
Verification ensures that mission outcomes are real, measurable, and aligned with the proposal.

Verification may include:
- On‑site documentation  
- Third‑party validation  
- Beneficiary confirmation  
- Photographic or video evidence  
- Receipts and financial logs  
- GPS‑tagged data (optional)  

Verification results must be included in the Impact Report.

---

### 6. Impact Reporting
After mission completion, the mission team must submit an official Impact Report using the template:  
→ [IMPACT_REPORT_TEMPLATE.md](/IMPACT_REPORT_TEMPLATE.md)

The report must include:
- Summary of activities  
- Quantitative and qualitative impact  
- Verification evidence  
- Budget reconciliation  
- Lessons learned  
- Recommendations  

Impact Reports are public and permanently archived.

---

### 7. Post‑Mission Audit
Governance Maintainers conduct an audit to ensure:

- Budget accuracy  
- Verification integrity  
- Compliance with mission objectives  
- Proper documentation  

If discrepancies are found:
- Additional evidence may be requested  
- Treasury safeguards may be triggered  
- Future mission approvals may be restricted  

---

### 8. Archival
Completed missions are archived for transparency and historical record.

Archive location:  
`/missions/archive/`

Archived items include:
- Mission proposal  
- Approval record  
- Execution logs  
- Impact report  
- Audit summary

## Mission Budget Rules

### Budget Requirements
All mission budgets must include:
- Itemized cost breakdown  
- Vendor or supplier information (if applicable)  
- Contingency allocation (recommended 5–10%)  
- Justification for each expense  

### Disbursement Rules
- Funds may be released in phases  
- Multi‑sig approval is required for treasury transfers  
- Emergency disbursements must be documented and reviewed post‑mission  

### Financial Transparency
All mission‑related expenses must be documented with:
- Receipts  
- Invoices  
- Transaction hashes (if on‑chain)  
- Logs of cash‑based operations (if applicable)

---

## Security Requirements
To protect mission integrity:

- Sensitive beneficiary data must be redacted  
- High‑risk missions require additional review  
- Treasury access must follow multi‑sig rules  
- Field teams must follow safety protocols  
- Emergency deviations must be logged and justified  

---

## Amendments to This Process
Changes to the Mission Process require:

- A Governance Proposal  
- Review by Governance Maintainers  
- Approval threshold defined in the Governance Framework  
- Public documentation of the amendment
