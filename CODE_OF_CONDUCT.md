# CODE OF CONDUCT  
*Standards of behavior for contributors and community members of the KAIROX humanitarian blockchain initiative.*

---

## 1. Purpose  
The purpose of this Code of Conduct is to ensure a safe, respectful, and professional environment for all contributors, community members, and participants in the KAIROX ecosystem.  
All interactions must reflect integrity, transparency, and the humanitarian mission of the project.

For security‑related issues, refer to:

- [PROPOSALS_OVERVIEW.md](./PROPOSALS_OVERVIEW.md)

---

## 2. Expected Behavior  
All participants must:  
- Act with professionalism and respect  
- Communicate clearly and constructively  
- Collaborate in good faith  
- Follow project guidelines and governance processes  
- Protect sensitive information  
- Uphold the humanitarian values of KAIROX  

---

## 3. Unacceptable Behavior  
The following behaviors are strictly prohibited:  
- Harassment, discrimination, or abusive conduct  
- Personal attacks or threats  
- Hate speech or derogatory language  
- Sharing private or confidential information  
- Disruptive or intentionally harmful actions  
- Fraud, deception, or misrepresentation  
- Any behavior that undermines the integrity of the project  

---

## 4. Reporting Violations  
If you witness or experience a violation of this Code of Conduct, report it privately to the maintainers or governance team.  
Reports will be handled confidentially and professionally.

Security‑related issues must **not** be reported here.  
Use the process described in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).

---

## 5. Enforcement  
Violations may result in:  
- Warnings  
- Temporary or permanent removal from discussions  
- Revocation of contributor privileges  
- Rejection of proposals or pull requests  
- Removal from the project in severe cases  

All enforcement decisions are made by the governance team.

---

## 6. Scope  
This Code of Conduct applies to:  
- All project repositories  
- All communication channels  
- All governance processes  
- All community interactions  
- Any official KAIROX activity  

---

## 7. Acknowledgment  
By participating in the KAIROX ecosystem, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct and uphold the values of the project.
