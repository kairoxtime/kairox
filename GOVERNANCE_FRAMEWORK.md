# KAIROX — Governance Framework  
Institutional Rules for Decision‑Making, Voting, and Stewardship

---

## Purpose of This Document
This Governance Framework defines how decisions are made within the KAIROX ecosystem.  
It establishes clear rules for:

- Proposal submission  
- Review and evaluation  
- Voting procedures  
- Approval thresholds  
- Roles and responsibilities  
- Transparency and accountability  

This framework ensures that KAIROX remains a transparent, community‑aligned, and mission‑driven humanitarian project.

## Governance Principles
KAIROX governance is based on the following principles:

1. **Transparency** — All decisions, proposals, and votes must be publicly accessible.  
2. **Accountability** — Contributors and maintainers are responsible for their actions.  
3. **Neutrality** — Governance decisions must prioritize humanitarian impact over personal or political interests.  
4. **Security** — All processes must minimize risk to treasury, missions, and community members.  
5. **Efficiency** — Governance must be lightweight, predictable, and easy to follow.

---

## Governance Roles

### 1. Contributors
Individuals who submit proposals, mission reports, or improvements.  
Responsibilities:
- Follow templates and naming conventions  
- Provide complete and accurate information  
- Respond to reviewer questions  

### 2. Governance Maintainers
Trusted members responsible for reviewing proposals.  
Responsibilities:
- Validate proposal completeness  
- Ensure alignment with mission and security standards  
- Approve proposals for voting  
- Maintain documentation and templates  

### 3. Council (Optional Future Layer)
A group of elected or appointed stewards.  
Responsibilities:
- Vote on high‑impact proposals  
- Approve treasury allocations  
- Oversee mission execution  

### 4. Community Members
Participants who provide feedback, discussion, and non‑binding sentiment.  
Responsibilities:
- Engage respectfully  
- Surface risks or concerns  
- Support transparency

## Types of Proposals

### 1. Mission Proposals
Requests to execute humanitarian missions.  
Examples:
- Water filtration deployment  
- Medical supply distribution  
- Emergency response initiatives  

### 2. Governance Proposals
Changes to rules, processes, or governance structure.  
Examples:
- Updating voting thresholds  
- Modifying contributor roles  
- Adjusting mission evaluation criteria  

### 3. Operational Proposals
Internal improvements to KAIROX systems.  
Examples:
- Documentation updates  
- Process automation  
- Infrastructure changes  

### 4. Treasury Proposals
Requests for funding or resource allocation.  
Examples:
- Mission budgets  
- Tooling expenses  
- Contributor compensation

## Proposal Lifecycle

### 1. Draft
The author submits a proposal using the official template:  
→ [KAIROX_PROPOSAL_TEMPLATE.md](/KAIROX_PROPOSAL_TEMPLATE.md)

File naming convention:  
`proposal-short-title-YYYY-MM-DD.md`

### 2. Review
Governance Maintainers evaluate:
- Completeness  
- Alignment with mission  
- Security considerations  
- Budget justification (if applicable)  

Maintainers may request revisions.

### 3. Vote
Once approved for voting, the proposal enters the decision phase.  
Voting methods may include:
- Council vote  
- Token‑based vote (future)  
- Multi‑sig approval  

### 4. Decision
A proposal is marked as:
- **Approved**  
- **Rejected**  
- **Deferred** (requires more information)  

### 5. Execution
If approved:
- Missions are deployed  
- Treasury funds are allocated  
- Operational changes are implemented  

### 6. Archive
Completed or rejected proposals are moved to:  
`/proposals/archive/`

## Voting Rules

### Quorum
Minimum participation required for a valid vote.  
Default: **60% of eligible voters** (Council or Maintainers).

### Approval Threshold
Percentage required for approval.  
Default: **≥ 66% yes votes**.

### Voting Duration
Standard voting window: **72 hours**.  
Extensions allowed for:
- Emergency proposals  
- High‑impact treasury requests  

### Emergency Voting
For urgent humanitarian missions:  
- Maintainers may bypass standard quorum  
- Minimum approval: **2/3 Maintainers**  
- Must be documented and justified

## Transparency Requirements

### All proposals must include:
- Clear title  
- Purpose  
- Expected impact  
- Risks and mitigations  
- Budget (if applicable)  
- Timeline  
- Responsible parties  

### All decisions must be:
- Public  
- Timestamped  
- Linked to the proposal file  
- Archived after completion  

---

## Security Requirements
To protect the ecosystem:

- No proposal may bypass treasury safeguards  
- All mission budgets must include verification steps  
- Sensitive data must be redacted  
- Multi‑sig approval required for treasury disbursements  
- Emergency actions must be logged and reviewed post‑execution  

---

## Amendments to This Framework
Any changes to this Governance Framework require:

- A Governance Proposal  
- Review by Maintainers  
- Approval threshold of **≥ 75%**  
- Public documentation of the change
