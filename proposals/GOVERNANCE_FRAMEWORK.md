## Purpose of This Document
This Governance Framework defines the structural evolution of the KAIROX ecosystem. It establishes the rules for:

- Proposal submission  
- Review and evaluation  
- Voting procedures  
- Approval thresholds  
- Roles and responsibilities  

**Note:** The protocol is currently in its **Bootstrap Phase**. Governance is founder-led to ensure rapid deployment and security during the foundational stage. Full community voting mechanisms will activate upon the transition to KRX2.

## Governance Principles
KAIROX governance is built on:

1. **Transparency** — All decisions and fund movements are publicly accessible.  
2. **Accountability** — Clear responsibility for mission execution and treasury safety.  
3. **Neutrality** — Prioritizing humanitarian impact over speculative interests.  
4. **Security** — Gradual decentralization to minimize risks to the treasury.

---

## Governance Roles (Phased Implementation)

### 1. Founder / Maintainer (Current Phase)
During the Bootstrap Phase, O. KAIROX acts as the primary steward to ensure alignment with the humanitarian mission and technical security.

### 2. Contributors
Individuals who submit mission proposals or documentation improvements.

### 3. Community Members
Token holders and supporters who provide feedback and sentiment via official channels (Telegram/X).

---

## Proposal Lifecycle

1. **Draft:** Author submits using the [Proposal Template](./KAIROX_PROPOSAL_TEMPLATE.md).  
2. **Review:** Validation of mission alignment and budget feasibility.  
3. **Internal Audit:** Verification of "Impact Fee" availability.  
4. **Execution:** Deployment of missions and on-chain reporting.  
5. **Archive:** All records are moved to `/proposals/archive/` for public audit.

---

## Voting & Thresholds (Future KRX2 Implementation)

- **Standard Quorum:** Minimum participation of **60%** of eligible voters.  
- **Approval Threshold:** Required **≥ 66%** "Yes" votes for standard proposals.  
- **Emergency Actions:** 2/3 Maintainer approval for urgent humanitarian response.

---

## Security & Transparency
- All mission budgets require verification steps and on-chain proof via Solscan.  
- Multi‑sig approval is the target standard for all treasury disbursements as the protocol scales.  
- Sensitive beneficiary data is redacted to maintain dignity and safety.
