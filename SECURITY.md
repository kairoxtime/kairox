# Security Policy
[![Security: Auditable](https://img.shields.io/badge/Security-Auditable-green.svg)](./SECURITY.md)
*Responsible disclosure guidelines for the KAIROX humanitarian blockchain initiative.*

---

## 1. Protocol Status: Bootstrap Phase
KAIROX is currently in its foundational stage. During this phase, security and treasury management are prioritized to ensure a stable transition to the full humanitarian operational model (KRX2). All security measures are managed directly by the Founder to ensure maximum responsiveness.

---

## 2. Reporting a Vulnerability
If you discover a security vulnerability within the KAIROX ecosystem, you must report it responsibly and privately.

### **How to Report:**
- **Primary Contact:** Reach out to the Founder (O. KAIROX) via official Telegram or X (Twitter) channels.
- **Details:** Include a description of the issue, steps to reproduce, and relevant logs or screenshots.
- **Confidentiality:** Do **not** disclose the vulnerability publicly before it is resolved to protect the Impact Fees.

We will acknowledge receipt within 48-72 hours.

---

## 3. Responsible Disclosure Requirements
To protect the project and its users, all reporters must follow these practices:
- Do not exploit the vulnerability for personal gain.
- Do not access or modify private data.
- Do not disrupt protocol services.
- Do not share the vulnerability with third parties before resolution.

## 5. Treasury Security
The KAIROX treasury is protected by:
- **Transparency:** All movements are visible via the [Official Solana Explorer](https://solscan.io/token/8Euwsi55sYUAQVXWro3Kt7oCK4EKaPvencw9CjrTpump).
- **Verification:** Every transaction must be linked to a mission or operational milestone documented in `/missions/`.
- **Roadmap:** Implementation of Multi-sig authority is the target standard as the protocol scales into KRX2.

---

## 6. Incident Response
In the event of a security incident:
1. Immediate assessment by the Founder and Core Maintainers.
2. Emergency governance procedures may be activated.
3. A public incident report (Post-Mortem) will be published after the threat is neutralized.

---

## 7. Policy Updates
This policy evolves alongside the KAIROX ecosystem. Significant changes will be documented and announced via official governance channels.
