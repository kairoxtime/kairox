# KAIROX — Treasury Policy (v1)

## 1. Executive Summary
This Treasury Policy defines the management, custody, and disbursement of the KAIROX ecosystem funds. The primary objective is to ensure that every unit of value is utilized for humanitarian impact and protocol sustainability, maintaining radical on-chain transparency.

## 2. Operational Status & Custody
### 2.1 Foundational Phase (Current: Pump.fun)
KAIROX is currently in its foundational stage on Pump.fun. During this phase, liquidity is governed by the bonding curve. No direct treasury management is active until the protocol graduates and migrates to its official infrastructure as outlined in the [Whitepaper v1](./WHITEPAPER_V1.md).

### 2.2 Post-Migration Institutional Custody
* **Hardware-Grade Security:** Upon graduation, the primary administrative keys will reside in a **Ledger** hardware wallet.
* **Multisig Framework:** A multi-signature structure (via **Squads Protocol**) will be implemented. Any treasury movement will require multiple approvals according to the thresholds defined by governance.

## 3. Revenue Generation (Impact Fees)
To ensure long-term sustainability and consistent humanitarian funding, KAIROX implements the following fee structure (to be activated upon KRX2 deployment):

| Fee Type | Percentage | Destination |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Impact Fee (Trade)** | 1.0% | Automatically routed to Treasury |
| **Staking Reward Tax** | 5.0% | Reinvested into Humanitarian Fund |
| **Corporate Grants** | 0.0% | 100% focused on Mission execution |

## 4. Fund Allocation (The 70/20/10 Rule)
Once the operational treasury is formed, all inflows will be distributed as follows:

* **70% — Humanitarian Fund:** Reserved exclusively for the execution of documented missions in the [missions](../missions/) folder.
* **20% — Operational Fund:** Allocated for infrastructure costs, protocol development, and maintenance of communication channels.
* **10% — Reserve Fund:** An emergency fund to ensure long-term liquidity and protocol stability.

## 5. Spending Limits & Thresholds
To prevent fund misuse, the following limits are established:
* **Micro-Disbursements:** Minor operational expenses (e.g., server fees) may be executed by the founder subject to subsequent audit.
* **Major Mission Funding:** Any expenditure exceeding **5% of the total treasury** must undergo a governance review cycle as defined in the [Governance Framework](../GOVERNANCE_FRAMEWORK.md).

## 6. Transparency & Auditing
* **Public Dashboard:** All treasury addresses are public and linked in the [INDEX.md](../INDEX.md) of the repository.
* **Real-Time Tracking:** Participants are encouraged to monitor flows through Solscan or dedicated blockchain explorers.
* **Institutional Audits:** At the end of each phase in the [Roadmap v1](./ROADMAP_V1.md), an account reconciliation will be performed and published as an Audit Report in the [archive](../archive/) folder.

## 7. Prohibited Activities
The KAIROX Treasury is strictly prohibited from:
* Engaging in high-risk lending (DeFi Lending) with humanitarian funds.
* Executing unauthorized token buybacks.
* Utilizing funds for personal expenses unrelated to protocol operations.
